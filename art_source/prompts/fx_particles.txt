Pixel-art visual effect sprite sheet for a 2D side-view pixel-art mining game, chunky visible pixels, limited palette, hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients. On a flat pure magenta #FF00FF background. Three horizontal rows, large gaps between every sprite, all sprites in a row the same cell size. No text, no labels, no grid lines, no borders, no cell frames.
Row 1: a DUST PUFF animation, 6 frames left to right: a small round cloud of soft dust appears, billows bigger with lumpy cauliflower edges, then thins out and breaks apart into a few wisps. Draw it in NEUTRAL light greys only (white #f0f0f0, #c8c8c8, #a0a0a0, #787878 shading on the lower side) so it can be colour-tinted in game.
Row 2: 6 different SPARK sprites: tiny bright sparks and short streaks, star-shaped glints and a small 4-point flash, white-hot core #fff8e0 with yellow #ffd27a and orange #ff8a2a edges.
Row 3: 6 different small ROCK CHIP / debris sprites: irregular angular pebbles and flakes of rock, NEUTRAL greys with one light top-left highlight and a dark bottom-right edge, so they can be tinted.
